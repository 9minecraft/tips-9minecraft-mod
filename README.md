# Tips — Master Hidden Mechanics While Your World Loads

Tips 9minecraft Mod adds a rotating set of gameplay hints that appear at the bottom of the loading screen each time you load into a world - a simple way to surface mechanics, shortcuts, and tricks that players often miss. Every world load shows a different tip drawn from a large curated pool. The hints cover everything from crafting recipes and enchanting tricks to exploration strategies and combat advice.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Darkhax**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
